## 🔍 About Me

I am a **results-driven Business Analyst** with over 14 years of experience spanning business intelligence, financial analysis, performance reporting, operations management, and auditing. I specialise in translating complex data from engineering, manufacturing, and financial systems into clear, actionable insights that support strategic decision‑making.

Currently, I serve as a **Business Analyst / Report Leader** on a €4.5B green steel megaproject (IDOM Consulting), leading end‑to‑end reporting, data validation, and KPI dashboard development for the PMC team. I work across multidisciplinary stakeholders—from client (SSAB) to EPC contractors—to ensure data consistency, governance, and executive-level visibility.

*   📍 **Location:** Sweden (open to relocation)
*   🎓 **Education:** MSc in Business Administration (Linköping University) · BSc Accounting (Robert Gordon University) · CIMA Advance Diploma
*   🔭 **Current Focus:** Automated reporting, Power BI dashboards, and business process improvement on large‑scale infrastructure projects.
*   ⚡ **Core Strengths:** Business analysis, data validation, stakeholder management, and bridging the gap between raw system data and human decisions.
*   💬 **On a personal note:** I believe in leading with empathy, fostering inclusive teams, and creating opportunities for English‑speaking talent in Sweden. Let's build a more diverse and collaborative workforce.
